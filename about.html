About Fran

Francine “Fran” Miller opened Fran's Flowers in 1995 with a simple goal: make beautiful flowers feel friendly, local, and personal.

What started as a small neighborhood flower shop grew into a place where customers stop by for birthdays, celebrations, thank-you gifts, and everyday moments that deserve a little color.

Fran still believes the best bouquet is one that makes somebody smile. The shop also continues to give ten percent of each sale to efforts that fight homelessness across the Bay Area.
